# OmniLaptop 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty (POL-LAPTOP-STD-02)

Product category: Laptops.

## 1. Coverage period
This warranty covers the insured product for 12 months from the original purchase date shown on the purchase invoice. Incidents that happen after the coverage period ends, or before the purchase date, are not covered.

## 2. Deductible
A deductible of $50.00 applies to every approved repair claim and is subtracted from the repair cost.

## 3. Maximum coverage limit
The maximum amount payable for a single claim is $1500.00.

## 4. Depreciation
A depreciation rate of 10.0% is applied to the eligible amount after the deductible and the coverage limit.

## 5. Exclusions
This warranty does not cover damage or failures caused by:
- water damage
- cosmetic scratches
- software viruses
- unauthorized disassembly

## 6. Required documents
- Original purchase receipt or invoice: required.
- Diagnostic or repair report from an authorised technician: required.

## 7. Settlement and approval
The payout is the repair cost minus the deductible, capped at the coverage limit, minus depreciation. Every payout is a proposal until a human claims reviewer approves it.
